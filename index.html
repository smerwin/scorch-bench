How to Play

Take turns lobbing shells at each other. Last tank standing wins the round; most points after the final round wins the game.

  • Aim: drag anywhere on the battlefield — your turret points at your finger, and the farther you drag from your tank, the more power. Fine-tune with the ◀ ▶ and − + buttons (hold to repeat).
  • Power is capped by your health: a tank at 40% can fire at most 400.
  • Wind (top centre) pushes shells sideways.
  • Weapons: tap the weapon button to switch. Baby Missiles are free and infinite; everything else is bought in the shop between rounds.
  • Items: raise shields, use batteries, drive with fuel, arm guidance, toggle parachutes and contact triggers.
  • Targeting: tap an enemy tank to mark it as the target for guidance systems and Lazy Boy.
  • Walls change each round: shells may wrap around, bounce, or blow up at the edges.
  • Dirt falls. Tanks fall too — and falling hurts unless you own a parachute.
  • Keyboard: ←/→ angle, ↑/↓ power (Shift = ×10), Space fire, Tab next weapon.
